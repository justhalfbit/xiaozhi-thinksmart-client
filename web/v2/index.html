    Speaker volume is for talking, shared with the hardware keys and voice. Alarm volume is only for timers, alarms, and reminders: it switches over while ringing and restores after. Volume keys during a ring change that ring only.

    Alarms and reminders

    Clock is not synced yet. Alarms are paused until NTP finishes.
